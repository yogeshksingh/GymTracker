# Gym Tracker Project Statement

## Problem Statement

It’s easy to forget exactly what you lifted from one gym session to the next. Gym Tracker gives people a simple, no-fuss place to log their exercises, sets, reps, and weights, then look back at the details later whenever they want.

## Scope

Gym Tracker is a terminal-based app that stores Push, Pull, and Legs workouts on the user’s computer. It checks the entries, saves them in a JSON file, and lets people browse, search, and summarise their training history in a straightforward way.

## Target Users

This is designed for people who want a quick, offline way to track their strength training without needing a complicated app or an internet connection.

## High-Level Features

- Add workouts and exercises with sets, reps, and weight.
- View workout history and see the volume for each session.
- Calculate total training volume and review workout counts.
- Search logged exercises by name.
- Save and reload workout data using JSON.
- Validate workout categories and numeric values to keep entries sensible.
